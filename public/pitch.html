BFSI PROTOTYPE

Collections Review Desk

AI-assisted call review with a recorded supervisor decision

The problem

A collector's note says the customer promised to pay. The conversation shows a disputed amount and no commitment. A supervisor needs the evidence before acting.

The workflow

Sarvam transcribes the Hindi-English call. A reviewer checks the words and speaker roles. Sarvam 105B compares the accepted transcript with the note and policy.

The completed service

Code verifies quotations and payment addresses. The supervisor approves the next step; the app saves the decision and follow-up.

4/4

Synthetic case smoke checks passed with Sarvam 105B.

Supported concern, clean call, missing evidence and a changed conversation. This small check is not a production accuracy estimate.

A bank pilot

Review historical calls alongside supervisors. Measure missed concerns, false alarms and time to a documented decision. Integrate with the existing collections case system.

Commercial validation

Credgenics raised a $50m Series B and names ICICI among bank clients. Convin publishes a Kotak Life call-audit deployment. Banks and insurers already spend on this category.
